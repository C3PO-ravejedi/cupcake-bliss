A few little details.

How far ahead should I order?

For this shop concept, allow 48 hours for box requests and a week for celebrations. Your request isn’t confirmed until the bakery replies.

What about food allergies?

Menu recipes contain wheat, eggs, and dairy; some ingredients may contain nuts. This is a shared kitchen, not an allergen-free facility. Ask before ordering.

Can you make a party box?

Absolutely the idea. Tell us your date, guest count, and favorite flavors in the request below. Custom decoration would be quoted separately.
